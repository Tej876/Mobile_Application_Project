# PRODUCT.md — QueueLess

## Product Truth
- **Name:** QueueLess
- **Category:** Real-time Clinic & Outpatient Queue Dispatch System
- **Primary Audiences:**
  1. **Patients:** Outpatients booking a department token, checking live queue position (`#01`, `#02`), receiving turn notifications, and knowing their estimated consultation wait time without crowding a physical waiting room.
  2. **Clinic Staff (Admins):** Reception and clinical coordinators calling the next patient in line (`#01`), marking completed consultations (`served`) or `no_show`, and auditing queue activity.
- **Visitor Mode:** `Operate` (Task-focused clinical utility across Mobile and Web).
- **Voice & Tone:** Calm, direct, reassuring, and unambiguous. Controls name their exact action; status indicators use both color and clear text.
